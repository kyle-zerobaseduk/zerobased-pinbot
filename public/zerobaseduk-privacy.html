ZeroBasedUK PinBot V2 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

PinBot V2 is a private content scheduling tool operated by ZeroBasedUK for managing the operator's own business Pinterest accounts and content.

Information the app accesses

When a Pinterest account is connected, PinBot V2 may access information authorised by the account owner, including the account's boards, Pins and basic account information required to publish and manage content.

How information is used

Account data is used only to let the owner select boards, create and schedule Pins, avoid duplicate posting, keep posting history and review basic performance information. PinBot V2 does not sell Pinterest user data or build advertising profiles about other users.

Authentication data

OAuth access and refresh tokens are treated as confidential credentials and stored in the application's protected server environment rather than intentionally committed to the public source-code repository.

Third-party destinations

Content created through the tool may link to product or business pages operated through third-party services, including Etsy and Amazon. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Data sharing

PinBot V2 does not sell personal information. Account data accessed by the app is used for the account owner's scheduling and publishing workflow and is not provided to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention and deletion

The operator may disconnect an account and remove locally stored scheduling, product and posting-history data. OAuth credentials should be revoked or deleted when an account is disconnected or the service is no longer used.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and application data. Secrets and access tokens are stored as deployment secrets or environment variables rather than source code.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be raised with ZeroBasedUK through its business contact channels.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PinBot V2 develops or if its API usage changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
